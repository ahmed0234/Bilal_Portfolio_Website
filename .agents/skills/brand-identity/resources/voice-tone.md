# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines (Google Ads Expert)

When generating website copy, headlines, case studies, or calls-to-action, adhere strictly to this brand voice and tone.

---

## Brand Personality & Archetype

* **The Elite Performance Marketer:** Data-driven, commercially astute, confident, and ROI-focused.
* **Direct & Metric-Oriented:** Lead with quantifiable numbers, verified returns, and proven revenue outcomes.
* **Jargon-Free Clarity:** Translate complex bidding algorithms and attribution models into simple business growth metrics.
* **Authority Without Arrogance:** Expert confidence backed by real case studies, spend volume, and profit gains.

---

## Typography & Presentation Rules

* **Headings (Poppins):** High-impact, punchy, Title Case. Focus on outcomes and revenue transformation.
  - *Example:* "Scaling High-Growth Brands With Precision Google Ads"
* **Subheadings & Body (Inter):** Clear, conversational, active voice. Explain the mechanics behind the results.
* **Metrics Callouts:** High-contrast stat + concise descriptor (e.g., `4.8x Average ROAS`, `$12M+ Revenue Generated`, `-38% Cost Per Acquisition`).

---

## Grammar & Copy Mechanics

* **Action-Oriented Verbs:** Use active drivers (`Scale`, `Maximize`, `Optimize`, `Drive`, `Generate`, `Audit`).
* **Punctuation:** Never use multiple exclamation marks (!!). Let the statistics and case study metrics create the excitement.
* **CTAs (Calls to Action):** High-intent, zero-fluff conversion triggers:
  - *Good:* `Book a 1-on-1 Growth Audit`, `Review Case Studies`, `Claim Free Account Audit`
  - *Avoid:* `Click Here`, `Learn More`, `Submit`

---

## Terminology Guide (Performance Marketing)

| Avoid / Fluff Term | Recommended Alternative | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Drive traffic" | "Acquire high-intent buyers" | Value proposition |
| "Digital marketing guru" | "Google Ads Specialist / Media Buyer" | Professional title |
| "Boost results" | "Maximize ROAS / scale profitable revenue" | Performance claim |
| "Utilize" | "Use" / "Deploy" | General copy |
| "In order to" | "To" | Conciseness |
| "Secret hacks" | "Algorithmic optimization & negative matching" | Technical depth |
